Two triangles in a circle

C is the center of the circle. AB and DE are straight lines through C. Joining A to D and B to E makes two triangles, ACD and BCE. Are the two triangles equal? If they are, prove it. If they are not, prove why not.

Measuring shows you what is true in this picture, not why it is true. Remember Monday: a line can look straight and not be. Your proof has to give a reason for every step.

Drag A, B, D, or E around the circle (or select one and use the arrow keys). Prefer to build it yourself? Use GeoGebra Geometry.
